Notification Toolkit is made by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we"). This policy explains what the app stores, where it stores it, and what we collect. The short version: your notification history stays on your iPhone. We don't collect it, see it or share it.

## The short version

- Your notification history is stored only on your iPhone.
- It is not included in iCloud or computer backups, so it doesn't move to a new iPhone.
- We have no server and no accounts. The app sends nothing to us.
- The app has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.
- Apple handles all purchases. We never see your payment details.

## How the app gets notifications

Notification Toolkit can't read your notifications on its own. You choose which apps to capture. For each one, you add a shortcut and turn on its automation in the Shortcuts app, using the trigger "When I receive a notification from" set to that app. When a notification from that app arrives, Shortcuts passes it to the app's Log Notification action, which saves it on your iPhone.

The app only captures apps you set up, and only from the time you set them up. To stop capturing an app, turn off or delete its automation in Shortcuts. Pro users can also pause capture from Control Center.

## What the app stores on your iPhone

- **Captured notifications.** The details Shortcuts passes for each notification, such as the app it came from, its title, its text and when it arrived.
- **Snoozed notifications.** Which notifications you snoozed and when they should come back.
- **Your settings.** For example, which apps you set up, how long to keep history, whether Face ID lock is on, and widget options.
- **Per-app stats.** Counts worked out on your iPhone from your history.

Notifications can include information about other people, such as the name of someone who messaged you. That information stays on your iPhone with the rest of your history, and you can delete it at any time.

## Where it is stored and how it is protected

Everything is stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone.

- History is protected by iOS Data Protection, using the class "Complete Until First User Authentication". This means it is encrypted and can't be read after your iPhone restarts until you unlock it for the first time.
- The app uses only the encryption built into iOS. It adds no encryption of its own.
- You can turn on Face ID lock so the app asks for Face ID before showing your history.

Some features show your notifications outside the app, on your iPhone only:

- **Home Screen widget (Pro).** It hides notification text by default. If you choose to show text, anyone who can see your Home Screen can read it.
- **Snooze (Pro).** A snoozed notification comes back as a notification from Notification Toolkit. iOS shows it according to your notification settings, which may include the Lock Screen.
- **Shortcuts actions.** If you run a shortcut that uses the app's search actions, the results go to that shortcut. What happens next depends on the shortcut you built or added.

## Backups and new iPhones

Your notification history is excluded from iCloud Backup and from backups made with a computer. It can't be restored from a backup, and it doesn't move to a new iPhone.

Your settings, such as how long to keep history, may be included in backups. They contain no notification content.

## How long the app keeps your history

You choose how long the app keeps history: 7 days, 30 days, 90 days or forever. The free version keeps 7 days. Longer options need Pro. The app deletes notifications older than your choice automatically.

## How to delete your data

In the app, you can delete:

- one notification,
- all notifications from one app, or
- all history.

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. We can't recover deleted history, because we never had a copy.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app doesn't send your notifications, your settings or any information about how you use it to us or to anyone else. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app. Our App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

If you turn on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share crash reports and app usage statistics with us. Apple handles that under its own privacy policy. It doesn't give us your notification history.

## What we share or sell

Nothing. We have no data about you to share or sell.

## Purchases

Apple processes all purchases of Notification Toolkit Pro. We don't receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app checks with Apple on your iPhone to find out whether you have Pro. Apple gives us sales reports that don't identify you.

## Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights over personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to look up, export, correct or delete. Your data is on your iPhone, under your control. You can still contact us with any question.

## Children

Notification Toolkit is not directed at children under 13. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Future features

If we ever add a feature that sends notification content off your iPhone, such as an AI feature that uses an outside service, we will:

1. update this policy first,
2. tell you in the app what would be sent and to whom, and
3. ask for your explicit permission before anything is sent. The feature will stay off unless you turn it on.
