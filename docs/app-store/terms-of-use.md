## Recommendation: use Apple's Standard EULA

Use Apple's Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement (the "Standard EULA") as the app's license. Don't write a custom EULA for 1.0.

How: leave the License Agreement field in App Store Connect blank. Apple then applies the Standard EULA to the app automatically. Its address is https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/.

Why:

- **It fits the app.** The Standard EULA covers the license grant, permitted use, warranty disclaimer, limitation of liability, export rules and governing law. Custom terms usually exist to cover accounts, servers, user content shared with others, or a paid online service. Notification Toolkit has none of these.
- **It is already reviewed and familiar.** App Review and users know it. Apple keeps it current.
- **It costs less to maintain.** A custom EULA needs legal drafting, review and updates, and it must still meet Apple's minimum terms.
- **It meets Guideline 3.1.2.** Apps with auto-renewable subscriptions must link to their terms of use in the App Store metadata and in the app. A page that links to the Standard EULA does this.

When to revisit: if a later version adds accounts, sync, a server, or a feature that sends notification content to an outside service (such as AI), get a lawyer to decide whether custom terms are needed.

The points below explain how Notification Toolkit Pro is sold and billed. They add detail. They don't change Apple's Standard EULA. If anything here conflicts with it, the Standard EULA applies.

### Free and Pro

- **Free:** up to 3 apps, 7 days of history, and search.
- **Pro:** unlimited apps, history kept as long as you choose, snooze, the Home Screen widget, the Control Center toggle, and per-app stats.

### Ways to buy Pro

| Product | Type | Price (U.S.) | Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Notification Toolkit Pro (Yearly) | Auto-renewable subscription | $19.99 per year | 1 year, renews automatically. Includes a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. |
| Notification Toolkit Pro (Lifetime) | One-time purchase | $39.99 | No end date |

Prices in other countries are shown in the App Store before you buy. They may differ and may include tax.

### Billing for the yearly subscription

- Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
- If you start the free trial, you are charged when the trial ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.
- The subscription renews automatically each year unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current year.
- Renewal is charged within the 24 hours before the current year ends, at the price shown in the App Store.

### Managing and canceling

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions. Canceling stops the next renewal. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

### Refunds

Apple handles all payments and refunds. To ask for a refund, go to https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

### Restoring Pro on a new iPhone

Pro is tied to your Apple Account. Use Restore Purchases in the app to turn it on again on a new iPhone, or after reinstalling.

Your notification history is not part of your purchase. It is stored only on your iPhone and is not included in iCloud or computer backups, so it doesn't move to a new iPhone. See the [Privacy Policy]([PRIVACY POLICY URL]).

### How capture works

Notification Toolkit captures notifications only from apps you set up in Shortcuts, and only from the time you set them up. It can't capture notifications from before setup. If an automation is turned off, or if iOS doesn't run it, those notifications are not saved.
